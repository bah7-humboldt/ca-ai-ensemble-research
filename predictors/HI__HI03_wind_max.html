What it measuresHI

The strongest island-average 10 m wind of the fire day.

Why it matters for fireHI

Strong trades drive Hawaii's fastest grass fires.

Hawaii has no Predictive Service Areas, so the islands are grouped as Kauai, Oahu, Maui Nui (Maui, Molokai, Lanai) and Hawaii Island. A large-fire day is a day when satellite-mapped growth, or the final size of the fires found that day, reached 300 acres. Hawaii's grass fires mostly burn out within a day or two, often between satellite passes, so the fire records (FPA FOD, InFORM) carry most of the history.
