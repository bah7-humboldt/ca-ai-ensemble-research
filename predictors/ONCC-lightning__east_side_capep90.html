Northeast California, Northern Sierra and Far Eastside thunderstorm setup

Thunderstorms and new lightning fires · Northern California, May–Oct · footprint: Northeast California, Northern Sierra and Far Eastside · today's run 2026-10-17 10:25 PT

What it measures

CAPE (convective available potential energy, J/kg) is the energy a rising air parcel can gain: the fuel for thunderstorms. This measure is the 90th-percentile CAPE across the footprint's grid cells, the higher of the fire day's 5 a.m. and 5 p.m. (12 and 00 UTC) values. "90th percentile" means the most unstable tenth of the footprint, where storms would form first.

Why it matters for fire

Lightning starts most of California's largest fires. In Northern California 235 of the 462 fires since 2001 that burned at least 1,000 acres were lightning-caused. Lightning fires come in clusters, often in remote country, and most are found on the day of the storm or the next. This row asks two things: will there be a lightning day, and how likely is a new large fire?

It was fit to 25 years of lightning strikes. Of the rules tested, this plain CAPE measure matched real lightning days best. Requiring dry air aloft made it worse: storms need moisture to form, and dryness matters for whether a strike starts a fire, which the fuels side (ERC) handles.

A lightning day here is a fire day with at least 100 strikes inside the footprint's PSAs. On a typical May–Oct day 13.9% of days are lightning days; per year that's about May 4.5, Jun 5.1, Jul 6.2, Aug 5.2, Sep 3.1, Oct 1.3 by month. Past the elevated cutoff, 53% are, and 31% have 500 strikes or more. History 2001-2025, 4,600 days, 828 past the cutoff.

New large lightning fires

On the 828 days past the cutoff, a lightning fire that went on to burn at least 1,000 acres was found in the footprint that day or the next on 57 of them (6.9%, against 2.5% on a typical May–Oct day): about ×2.5 the usual odds for the date. That covers 50% of the 115 days a large lightning fire was found there.

ERC is the footprint-average energy release component percentile from the toolkit's NFDRS reference data (2005–present). Each cell's odds are pulled toward its row's overall odds by 40 days' worth of history, so thin cells can't swing the forecast.

Dry versus wet lightning: on the 478 lightning days with under 0.1 inch of rain across the footprint (ERA5), a large lightning fire was found that day or the next on 13.4% of them; on the 159 wetter lightning days, 1.9%.

The biggest lightning-fire days

Days the most large (≥1,000-acre) lightning fires were found in the footprint, 2001–2025, with the strikes that day or the day before and what this measure showed.

  • Sat Jun 21, 2008: 5,500 strikes in the footprint, the thunderstorm setup was 133 J/kg (past the cutoff), ERC at the 82nd percentile. 12 large lightning fires found: Long Branch-Jack (Btu Lightning Complex), Scotch (Canyon Complex), Cub Complex, Government (American River Complex) and others (144,933 acres in all).
  • Sun Aug 2, 2009: 4,548 strikes in the footprint, the thunderstorm setup was 762 J/kg (past the cutoff), ERC at the 93rd percentile. 4 large lightning fires found: Chalk (Shu Lightning Complex), Cassel (Shu Lightning Complex), Goose (Shu Lightning Complex), Brown (Hat Creek Complex) (19,381 acres in all).
  • Thu Aug 9, 2001: 2,508 strikes in the footprint, the thunderstorm setup was 11 J/kg (below the cutoff). 3 large lightning fires found: Observation, Blue Complex, Rock (Modoc Complex) (100,046 acres in all).
  • Mon Aug 17, 2020: 2,772 strikes in the footprint, the thunderstorm setup was 244 J/kg (past the cutoff), ERC at the 85th percentile. 2 large lightning fires found: North Complex, Sheep (North Complex) (345,680 acres in all).
  • Sat Aug 18, 2012: 1,034 strikes in the footprint, the thunderstorm setup was 109 J/kg (past the cutoff), ERC at the 94th percentile. 2 large lightning fires found: Bagley Complex, Ponderosa (76,381 acres in all).
  • Sun Aug 5, 2012: 284 strikes in the footprint, the thunderstorm setup was 2 J/kg (below the cutoff), ERC at the 94th percentile. 2 large lightning fires found: Lost, Lake Complex (59,765 acres in all).
