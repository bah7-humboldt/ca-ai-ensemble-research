Easterly trade wind upstream (850 hPa)

Calibrated predictor · Hawaii, Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec · all islands · today's run 2026-10-17 10:25 PT

What it measures

The fire day's highest value of the easterly part of the 850 hPa wind averaged over the trade-wind box northeast of the islands (20–24°N, 158–153°W), from ERA5 for the history and from each ensemble for the forecast.

Why it matters for fire

A single measure of how strong (or dry) the trades feeding the islands are, free of any one station's terrain.

Hawaii has no Predictive Service Areas, so the islands are grouped as Kauai, Oahu, Maui Nui (Maui, Molokai, Lanai) and Hawaii Island. A large-fire day is a day when satellite-mapped growth, or the final size of the fires found that day, reached 300 acres. Hawaii's grass fires mostly burn out within a day or two, often between satellite passes, so the fire records (FPA FOD, InFORM) carry most of the history.

The biggest fire days and what this measure showed

  • Fri Jul 30, 2021: Easterly trade wind upstream (850 hPa) was 9.1 m/s (not past the cutoff), with ERC at the 62nd percentile. Fires that day that reached 300+ acres: Mana Road Fire on Hawaii Island, 40,000 acres in all.
  • Wed Aug 1, 2018: Easterly trade wind upstream (850 hPa) was 11.2 m/s (past the cutoff), with ERC at the 70th percentile. Fires that day that reached 300+ acres: Waikoloa Fire, Highway 190 Fire on Hawaii Island, 33,287 acres in all.
  • Mon Aug 1, 2005: Easterly trade wind upstream (850 hPa) was 9.7 m/s (not past the cutoff), with ERC at the 85th percentile. Fires that day that reached 300+ acres: unnamed on Hawaii Island, 25,000 acres in all.
  • Thu Jul 21, 2022: Easterly trade wind upstream (850 hPa) was 7.9 m/s (not past the cutoff), with ERC at the 73rd percentile. Fires that day that reached 300+ acres: Leilani on Hawaii Island, 17,711 acres in all.
  • Thu Jul 11, 2019: Easterly trade wind upstream (850 hPa) was 7.4 m/s (not past the cutoff), with ERC at the 55th percentile. Fires that day that reached 300+ acres: Emergency Response Waiko Fire, Central Maui on Maui Nui, 16,858 acres in all.
